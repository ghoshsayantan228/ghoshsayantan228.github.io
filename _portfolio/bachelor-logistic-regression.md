## Overview

A Bachelor's project in Statistics focused on identifying health parameters
associated with diabetes among women and building a statistical model to
estimate diabetes risk.

## Approach

- Analysed a dataset containing 376 women and 8 variables.
- Performed exploratory analysis of seven health-related predictors,
  including glucose, blood pressure, BMI, insulin and diabetes pedigree
  function.
- Modelled the binary diabetes outcome using a generalized linear model
  with a logit link.
- Compared model specifications using significance, AIC and residual
  deviance.
- Removed less-contributing variables to obtain a more parsimonious model.

## Findings

The final model identified pregnancies, glucose level, blood pressure,
insulin, BMI and diabetes pedigree function as significant predictors in
the dataset.
